# Okwan

US visa interview preparation built for Ghanaians. You upload your documents, then practise with a simulated consular officer who has read your case. After each session you get an honest debrief grounded in your own facts.

> Okwan means *the way / the road* in Twi.

The full product, engineering, design and SEO plan, with sources, is in [`docs/PLAN.md`](docs/PLAN.md).
